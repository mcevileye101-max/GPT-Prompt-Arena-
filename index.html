Questions

Is it free?

Browsing, copying and publishing prompts are free.

Where are my likes and collections stored?

On this device, in your browser. They are not shared across devices yet.

What can I upload?

Images up to 10 MB and videos up to 100 MB, together with the prompt that created them.

What are placeholders?

If a prompt contains parts in [square brackets] or {curly braces}, the prompt page lets you fill them in and copy your version.

Which models are shown?

Model badges such as Midjourney or DALL·E appear when the prompt text mentions them.

Help and shortcuts

Everything you need to get the most out of Prompt Arena.

How do I use a prompt?

Open any card, tap Copy Prompt, and paste it into your AI tool. Tap Try in ChatGPT to open it there directly.

How do I publish my own prompt?

Go to Create, add a title, your full prompt and the image or video it produced, then press Publish.

What are collections?

Folders for your favorite prompts. Open a prompt, tap Save, and pick or create a collection. Find them under User, Collections.

What is Prompt Studio?

A builder that turns a subject, style, lighting and camera choices into a ready prompt. Send it straight to Create.

Where is my data stored?

Likes, collections, history and your name live in this browser. Use Settings, Export to back them up.
